# Turf Instrument

**A design system of cool measurement.**

Turf Instrument treats coaching like instrumentation: crisp, athletic, and
honest about what a phone can see. The field is a cool mist green-gray — early
range fog, not brochure cream — and the ink is a deep forest green read as turf
from altitude. One kinetic accent is permitted: a warm amber signal that marks
the thing that moves. Still green describes what *is*; amber describes what
*happens*. That chromatic grammar stays intact from the Shopify storefront
through `app.caddieinsight.com`.

The Tour Caddie v3 mark is a precision arc around a fairway flagstick — golf
plus measurement in one glyph — with the amber segment as the only kinetic
stroke. Membership photography stays photoreal and scene-distinct (dawn range
for Pro, private-club dusk for Founders) without sticker overlays on the media.

Typography is geometric and purposeful. **Archivo** carries the
storefront voice via Shopify's font CDN. The app shell may load a sibling
geometric face (**Sora**) with **IBM Plex Mono** for measured evidence:
pass marks, specimen labels, chip copy, and calibration notes. Nothing
decorative explains the product; the artifact does.

Form stays soft-rectilinear. Controls use confident twelve-to-sixteen-pixel
radii, never pill clusters. Cards appear only when they hold an interaction or
a comparable choice; elsewhere, hairlines and open space do the work. Full-bleed
heroes keep the brand as a hero-level signal, one headline, one supporting
sentence, and one CTA group over a dominant range photograph.

Rhythm is systematic: shared tokens for color, type, radius, elevation, and
section spacing so the storefront hub and the app read as one continuous
instrument. Motion is sparse and intentional — scroll reveal, a live signal
pulse, and CTA arrow travel — never noise.

Craftsmanship remains the ethic. Optical alignment beats grid perfection. Work
that looks hurried is discarded. Work that looks inevitable, because it was
slow, is kept.
